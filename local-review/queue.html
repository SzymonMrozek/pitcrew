Waiting merges start preparation after the current merge finishes. Blocked items need attention and let the queue continue.

Agent workflow

Start with --queue. Compare pending changed paths with your task, including shared interfaces and callers. Unrelated work can start from the current target.

For overlapping work, inspect the saved snapshot ref and incorporate the relevant pending changes in your own worktree. The snapshot includes uncommitted approved files; it can change during conflict resolution. Wait for dependencies to land before approving your own full diff.

Use the review’s Approve & merge action. Its worker waits for a turn, then updates the target, rebases, handles comments and executes the same approval. Merge checks and Worktrunk hooks validate and clean up; do not duplicate them beforehand.

Inspect an approval with --merge-request <id>. Resume a stopped worker from its existing worktree with --resume-merge-request <id>.
